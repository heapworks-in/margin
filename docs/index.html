Your toughest reviewer, before your real ones.

Margin is a desktop app for design docs, RFCs, ADRs and postmortems. You write; a senior reviewer's notes land on your text as underlines; a panel scores the document; you rehearse the review meeting in private. Your words stay on your Mac.

brew tap heapworks-in/tap && brew install --cask margin
Apple Silicon, macOS 13+. Or the DMG. Uses your Claude Code login, or Devin, Codex, Gemini CLI, Goose if you have them. No API key.

Notes that live on the text

Underlines by kind: English, missing, fact, question, structure. Click one for the fix, accept it in place. Every quote is checked against your words before it reaches you, so the coach cannot make things up about your document.

A panel, a rubric, a rehearsal

Three reviewers chosen for the document type score eight dimensions and say SHIP or REVISE. Then they ask you their questions one at a time and grade your answers. The review meeting, practised in private.

It learns your reviewers, and teaches you

Paste the comments you really got and the coach recalibrates. Your repeated English mistakes become short lessons. History shows each document getting better, round by round.

One document, step by step

The real screens, in the order you would use them. Click a step, or use ← and →.

Try it in your browser

The same editor, running here with a scripted coach instead of a real one. Click the underlines, open the rubric, drag blocks, press /. Nothing you type leaves the page. The desktop app adds the real coach, your workspace on disk, and import and publish.
